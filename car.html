Before you buy

Questions to ask.

  • Which market specification is this car, and does it match the charging stations you will use?
  • Who provides the warranty in Georgia, and what exactly does it cover?
  • Is the battery covered separately, and on what terms?
  • Who services the car locally, and are parts stocked or ordered?
  • Which software and connectivity features work in Georgia?
