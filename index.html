Selling electric cars? Let's make a video about yours.

EVebi is a Georgian channel about electric and plug-in hybrid cars. You give me a car or invite me to an event, and I make a video that shows it honestly to people in Georgia who are thinking of buying one.

  • A video in Georgian for local buyers, covering what they actually ask: warranty, service, charging and real range.
  • A clear label on every video, either independent review or paid promotion, so viewers know what they are watching.
  • An honest test: I drive the car on real roads and say what is good and what is not. Payment does not buy a positive review.
