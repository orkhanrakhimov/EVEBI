Tesla Model Y

The new design, the cabin and the software are clearly better. The materials still let it down.

Watch the video

My honest take

A clear step forward from the previous Model Y. It looks modern, the cabin feels more premium and comfortable, and the software and the battery are still the best things about it. The weak spot is the quality of some materials.

What I liked

  • The new design is modern and, to me, better than the previous model. The back of the car looks especially good.
  • The trunk is quite spacious, with a lot of luggage room.
  • Beautiful ambient lighting and a new interior style.
  • New seats, now with ventilation.
  • A screen for rear passengers.
  • The car can understand in most cases whether it should go forward or back.
  • The software and the screen are very capable, and the battery is great.

What I liked less

  • Changing gear on the screen felt weird at first, though you can get used to it.
  • The automatic forward and reverse detection needs improvement.
  • The quality of some materials in the car is still the worst thing about it.
What others say

Reported by other reviewers and owners. I did not test these.

Pros

  • The boot is praised.
  • Road noise is more muted than before.

Cons

  • Build quality: some owners report crooked lights, misaligned panels, rattles and scratches found at delivery.
  • The ride is firmer at speed than in many rivals, and tyre noise is still there on the motorway.
  • Few physical controls. Lights, wipers and gear selection are on the screen, and gear selection only appears when the car is stationary.
  • The indicator stalk feels cheap.
  • The rear window is small and shallow, and there is no rear wiper.
  • The cabin feels bare on the cheaper trim.

Sources: r/ModelY thread and Auto Express. Trim, market and factory can differ.
