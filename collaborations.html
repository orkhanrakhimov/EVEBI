Why work with EVebi.

Because showing a car honestly is worth more than showing it loudly.

1

Made for people choosing a car in Georgia

Content in Georgian that answers the questions buyers here actually ask: warranty, service and parts, charging, real range. Your car is presented to people who are thinking about an electric or plug-in hybrid, not to a general audience.

2

Trust that comes from being clear about who is talking

Everything on EVebi is labelled as independent, sponsored or dealership-provided. Because opinions are not for sale, viewers have a reason to believe what they watch, including what you commission.

3

A real look at the car, not a brochure

Cars are driven in everyday conditions, with the technology explained in plain words. Viewers see what the car does well, who it suits and where it has limits. That builds more confidence than a polished advert.

4

A direct conversation

You talk directly to EVebi, with no agency in between. A message on WhatsApp is enough to start, and the format, the label and where it is published are agreed before anything is filmed.

What you can and cannot expect

You can expect honest, well-made, clearly labelled content. You cannot buy a guaranteed positive verdict: paid content is marked as promotion, and independent reviews say what the car really does.

Cars, experienced for real.

01

Independent reviews

You provide the car; I drive it, explore the technology and say honestly what works and what doesn't. Independent reviews are labelled as such, and payment never buys a good verdict.

02

Vehicle presentations and promotional content

Clear, well-made videos that present a car you want to sell: what it offers, who it suits and how to buy it. Always labelled as promotion.

03

Road trips and real-world tests

Cars on real Georgian roads: mountain drives, range and charging in everyday conditions, long-distance comfort. Possible routes include Tbilisi to Kazbegi or Batumi. These are ideas for future projects, not completed work.

04

Events and model launches

Coverage of reveals, test-drive days, dealership openings and exhibitions, from the point of view of someone who actually drives the cars.

05

Content for your own channels

Short-form videos and automotive content produced for a dealership's or brand's social media.

06

Creative brand campaigns

Unconventional partnerships with dealerships, manufacturers, hotels, destinations, charging companies and technology brands.
