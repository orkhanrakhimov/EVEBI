Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal data EVebi handles when you visit evebi.ge or contact EVebi, why, and what your rights are. It is written to be read, so it is short and plain.

Who is responsible

EVebi is run by Orkhan Rakhimov in Tbilisi, Georgia. For anything about your data, write to contact@evebi.ge.

What this website collects

The site has no accounts, no contact forms, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may keep a simple display preference, such as language, on your own device; it is never sent to EVebi.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details when you load a page. EVebi does not receive or use this data. See GitHub's own privacy statement for how it handles it.

When you contact EVebi

The site offers WhatsApp and email links. When you use them, you send a message from your own app, and EVebi receives what you choose to include: your name or number, your email address and the content of your message.

  • Why it is used: to reply to you and to deal with your request, such as a collaboration, a question or help choosing a car.
  • Not sold: your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

WhatsApp is a service of Meta, and email is carried by your provider and the one EVebi uses. They handle messages under their own privacy terms.

How long data is kept

Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and then deleted. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

Content and collaborations

If a video, post or other content involves you or your business, what appears in it is agreed with you beforehand. Dealership and brand information on this site is shown with the trust labels explained on the About page.

Links to other services

Links to TikTok, WhatsApp and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies. EVebi is not responsible for how they handle your data.

Your rights

Under Georgian data protection law you can ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected or deleted, withdraw consent you gave, and object to how it is used. Write to contact@evebi.ge and the request will be answered. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia.

Children

The site is not directed at children, and EVebi does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. This page is general information about how EVebi works with data and is not legal advice.
