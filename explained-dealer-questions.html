What to ask a dealership before buying an EV

Buying an electric car, especially from a brand you don't know well, comes down to a few things the brochure rarely explains. Take these questions with you and ask for the answers in writing. If a dealership can't answer one, that is useful information too.

1. Which car exactly is this?

  • Which market is this car built for (for example European, Chinese or American specification)?
  • Is it officially distributed in Georgia, or imported separately? Who is the importer?
  • Model year, exact variant and battery size?
  • Which charging inlet does it have? (See the connectors guide.)

2. Warranty

  • Who issues the warranty: the manufacturer, the importer or the dealership?
  • How long does it last, and is there a distance limit?
  • Is the warranty valid in Georgia for this specific car? Can I see the document?
  • What is excluded, and what could void it?

3. Battery

  • Is the battery covered separately, and for how long or how far?
  • Does the warranty set a minimum battery capacity, and who measures it?
  • What happens if the battery needs repair or replacement, and who pays?

4. Servicing and parts

  • Who services the car in Georgia, and are the technicians trained for this brand?
  • Are common parts kept in stock, or ordered? How long does ordering usually take?
  • Is there a loan car or other support while mine is being repaired?

5. Software, charging and paperwork

  • Which features and apps work in Georgia, and in which language?
  • Is a charging cable included, and can the dealership help with a home charger?
  • What are the registration, customs and delivery steps, and who handles them?
  • Can I take a test drive?

Remember

Warranties, prices and availability are the dealership's and manufacturer's responsibility. EVebi does not provide warranties or guarantees and can't confirm a dealership's claims unless a page says it was independently checked.
