Cars, experienced for real.

01

Independent reviews

You provide the car; I drive it, explore the technology and say honestly what works and what doesn't. Independent reviews are labelled as such, and payment never buys a good verdict.

02

Vehicle presentations and promotional content

Clear, well-made videos that present a car you want to sell: what it offers, who it suits and how to buy it. Always labelled as promotion.

03

Road trips and real-world tests

Cars on real Georgian roads: mountain drives, range and charging in everyday conditions, long-distance comfort. Possible routes include Tbilisi to Kazbegi or Batumi. These are ideas for future projects, not completed work.

04

Events and model launches

Coverage of reveals, test-drive days, dealership openings and exhibitions, from the point of view of someone who actually drives the cars.

05

Content for your own channels

Short-form videos and automotive content produced for a dealership's or brand's social media.

06

Creative brand campaigns

Unconventional partnerships with dealerships, manufacturers, hotels, destinations, charging companies and technology brands.
