How I review.

Every car has a label

It says who provided the car or invited me, so you always know the context. Payment never buys a positive opinion.

My opinion is separate from other people's

"What I liked" and "What I liked less" are mine. "What others say" is reported by other reviewers and owners, and I did not test it.

If I did not drive it, I say so

A first impression is not a full review, and I will not pretend otherwise.
