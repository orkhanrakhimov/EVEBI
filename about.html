About EVebi

Independent EV media, built around a real person.

EVebi is a Georgian electric vehicle media brand founded by Orkhan Rakhimov in Tbilisi. The goal: make electric cars easier to understand, and easier to trust, for people in Georgia.

Why EVebi exists

Georgia's EV market has a lot of interest and too little clear information.

More people in Georgia are curious about Tesla, BYD, ZEEKR and other electric brands, but many are unsure, especially about Chinese cars. How long do batteries last? Who services them? What does a warranty really cover?

I'm an EV enthusiast, and I started making Georgian-language videos to answer those questions by actually driving and testing the cars, then explaining what I find without marketing language.

How I test cars

Real roads, real use.

01

Drive it

I spend time with the car in everyday conditions rather than reading a spec sheet aloud.

02

Look at the technology

Software, driver assistance, charging and the details that matter when you own the car.

03

Say what I think

Strengths and limitations, plainly. If something isn't confirmed, I say so.

Straight about me

An enthusiast, not an expert in a lab coat.

I'm not an automotive engineer or a certified technician, and I don't present myself as one. What I offer is hands-on experience, curiosity and honesty.

Where it's going

From content to a trusted EV destination.

Today EVebi is video content and the guides on this site. The aim is to grow into a trusted place in Georgia for EV information, dealership transparency, collaborations and buying assistance, one honest step at a time.

Editorial standards

How EVebi labels what you read and watch.

Independent editorial

My own opinion about a car, including its limitations.

Sponsored promotional

Paid or arranged content, clearly marked. Payment never guarantees a positive review.

Dealership-provided

Claims made by a dealership, shown as theirs.

Independently checked

Only used when specific verification has actually happened, and dated.

Not confirmed

Unverified information, labelled as such.
