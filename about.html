Independent EV media, made in Georgia.

EVebi is a Georgian electric vehicle media brand based in Tbilisi. EVebi covers fully electric cars and hybrids with a real electric drive, such as plug-in hybrids. The goal: make them easier to understand, and easier to trust, for people in Georgia.

What is changing in Georgia.

Cars in Georgia are shifting fast. The information people need to choose well has not kept up.

Electric and hybrid cars are becoming common

Hybrids, plug-in hybrids and fully electric cars made up 29% of the cars imported into Georgia in the first half of 2026, up from about 21% a year earlier. Fully electric imports grew by roughly 64% over the same period. These are preliminary figures from Geostat trade data, compiled by AutoBridge Data.

Chinese brands are arriving quickly

The value of passenger cars imported from China rose by about 76% in the first nine months of 2026, from roughly $40 million to $71 million, according to Georgia Today. Many of the brands are new to Georgian buyers, so there is little local experience to rely on.

Costs and rules are shifting

For many drivers, fuel costs are a reason to look at electric and hybrid cars. Tax rules are changing too: since April 2026, new excise rules mean electric cars carry no excise, while petrol and diesel cars pay by engine size.

Clear answers are still hard to find

People ask the same things again and again. How long do batteries last? Who services the car and supplies parts? What does a warranty really cover? Is a hybrid enough, or should it be fully electric? Is a Chinese car a safe choice? Most answers come from sellers, who have a reason to sound confident.

That is the gap EVebi is for.

EVebi makes Georgian-language videos and guides that look at electric and plug-in hybrid cars the way a buyer would: by driving them, looking at the technology and the practical details, and saying plainly what is confirmed and what is not.

How EVebi labels what you read and watch.
